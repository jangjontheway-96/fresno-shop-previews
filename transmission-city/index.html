Warning signs we fix every day

Catching a transmission problem early is the difference between a repair and a rebuild. Call if you notice any of these.

01

Slipping gears

RPMs jump but the car doesn't pick up speed.

02

Hard or late shifts

Clunks, jerks or a long pause before it engages.

03

Leaks or burnt smell

Red fluid under the car or a hot, burnt odor.

04

Warning lights

Check engine or transmission light on the dash.
